# Jomhor — brand

## Idea

Jomhor (جمهور) is "the crowd" — the fans, the 12th man. The mark is a J drawn with
twelve dots: eleven ink or white dots form the letter, the twelfth, in amber, sits at the
end of the hook — the supporter who completes the team. It reads as a J at 24 px and as
a crowd at 1024 px.

Jomhor is not white-label (founder, 2026-11-07): the product chrome is always Jomhor, ink + amber. A club appears as
content (its name and crest on its events, tickets and club pages), never as the app's identity, and its colours do
not re-skin the chrome.

## Type

- Wordmark: Inter Display ExtraBold, tracking −3.5%, delivered as paths. Do not retype it.
- Arabic wordmark: جمهور in Cairo ExtraBold, amber on ink, ink on light.
- Product UI: Barlow Condensed (display) + Inter (body); Arabic UI: Cairo + IBM Plex Sans Arabic.

## Rules

- Clear space: one dot diameter (≈ 11/120 of the tile) on every side.
- Minimum size: tile 24 px; lockup 120 px wide. Below that, mark only.
- The amber dot is always the one at the end of the hook; never recolour the other eleven.
- On amber backgrounds all twelve dots are ink.
- No gradients, shadows, outlines or rotation. Never place the mark inside another shape.
- Tagline (optional, never inside the lockup): "Jomhor. Le 12e homme." / "جمهور. الرجل الثاني عشر."

## Voice

Direct, warm, stadium-side. Darija-first when speaking to fans ("Dkhol, rak dakhel"),
French for organizers, no corporate filler. Numbers and names big; everything else small.
